## The short version
- Your topics, entries, notes and reminders are stored **only on your phone**. We never receive them.
- There are **no ads** and **no account**.
- If — and only if — you agree, the app sends **anonymous usage statistics** and **crash reports** to help us improve it. You can turn this off anytime in Settings.

## 1. Data stored on your device
Topics, calendar entries, notes, reminder times and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. You can export them yourself (CSV/JSON) from Settings → Backup.

## 2. Data collected with your consent
When you choose "Share anonymous stats", we use Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd, to collect:
- **App interactions** — e.g. "an entry was added", "the stats screen was opened", which period filter was used. Never the names of your topics, your notes, or the dates you log.
- **Crash logs and diagnostics** — device model, Android version, app version, and technical details of a crash.
- **App instance ID** — a random identifier created by Firebase for this installation. We do **not** collect the advertising ID.

**Purpose:** understand which features are useful and fix bugs (legal basis in the EU/UK: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
**Retention:** analytics data is kept for 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.
**Transfers:** Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its standard contractual clauses.

## 3. What we never do
- Show ads or use advertising identifiers
- Sell or share your personal information
- Read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or other apps

## 4. Your choices and rights
- **Turn off** analytics anytime: Settings → Privacy & analytics. Collection stops immediately.
- **Delete** analytics data: email privacy@orbitra.app with your request (we will delete data associated with your app instance where technically possible) or reinstall the app to reset the app instance ID.
- **EU/UK residents** have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object and to data portability, and may complain to their data protection authority.
- **California residents** (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 5. Children
Habit Tracker is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 6. Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to create them.

## 7. Changes
We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

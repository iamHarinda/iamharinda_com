# PeriodicElementTable.com — Brand Kit

## The mark: "the orbit P"
A lowercase **p** whose bowl is an electron orbit, with one Sodium-orange electron riding it.
Underneath sits the **flame-test bar** — five colours taken from real flame tests
(lithium, sodium, barium, copper, potassium). The bar is the brand's signature; the same five
colours reappear as the lit tiles in the periodic-table pattern on banners.

## Type
**Source Sans 3** (Google Fonts, free). Bold/Black for headlines and the wordmark, Regular for body.
Wordmark: `PeriodicElementTable` Bold + `.com` Regular in a lighter tone.

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the electron dot's diameter.
- 16–48 px: use the favicon version (no flame bar). 64 px and up: full mark.
- Don't recolour the electron or reorder the flame bar. Don't rotate or outline the mark.
- On photos, place the mark on its Cobalt tile, never bare.

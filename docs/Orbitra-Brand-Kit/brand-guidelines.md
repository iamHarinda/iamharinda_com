# Orbitra

**Many worlds, one orbit.**

Orbitra is an umbrella studio for mobile apps, web platforms, AI tools and games. Every product is its own world with its own name; Orbitra is the orbit that ties them together. The brand stays quiet and confident so the products can be loud.

## The idea

The mark is an open orbit ring with a single satellite riding in the gap: something always in motion, always coming back. It reads as an "O", as a planet with a moon, and as a loading/progress state, which suits tools, AI and games alike.

## Brand architecture

- **Master brand:** Orbitra (Play Store developer name, website, legal footer).
- **Products keep their own names** and carry an endorsement line set in `caption`: "by Orbitra". Example: ToolsServer, by Orbitra.
- **Divisions** when a category grows: Orbitra Games, Orbitra AI, Orbitra Labs. Always "Orbitra" + one plain English word.

## Voice

Clear, calm, a little playful. Short sentences, plain words, no hype.

- Do: "Resize a photo in two taps." / "Your files stay on your device."
- Don't: "Revolutionary AI-powered next-gen synergy!!!"
- Write the brand as **Orbitra** in sentences; the logo wordmark alone is lowercase **orbitra**.

## Colour

Warm neutrals with one hot orange. `solar` is identity: the mark, the one primary action on a screen, key highlights. Text on `solar` is always `ink`, never white. `solar-text` is the orange you use for links and text. `ion` (teal) is the rare cool counterweight for icons, charts and "new/success" states. `deep` grounds hero bands and the app icon. Pages sit on `surface`, cards on `surface-raised`, divided by `line`.

Rule of thumb for a screen: about 70% surface, 20% ink and neutrals, under 10% solar, a touch of ion.

## Typography

- **Space Grotesk** (`display`, `title`) for headlines and the wordmark.
- **Inter** (`heading`, `body`, `caption`) for all interface and reading text.
- **JetBrains Mono** (`code`) for code, URLs and version numbers.

All three are free on Google Fonts, so every team member and product can use them.

## Shape and space

Everything sits on an 8px rhythm (`space-2` to `space-8`). Corners are soft (`radius-md` for buttons and cards); anything that orbits or signals status is a full circle (`radius-full`). Borders use `line` hairlines rather than heavy shadows.

## Iconography

Line icons with a 2px stroke and round caps and joins, echoing the mark's ring. Use ink by default; `solar` only for the active state. Avoid filled glossy icons, emoji as icons, and blue-purple gradients.

## App icons for sub-products

Each product gets its own icon, but shares Orbitra's system: rounded square, a `deep` or `surface` ground, one simple geometric symbol, and `solar` or `ion` as the accent. This keeps the Play Store developer page looking like one family.

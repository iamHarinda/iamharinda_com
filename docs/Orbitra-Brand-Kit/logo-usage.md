# Logos

- `orbitra-mark.svg`: the primary mark in solar. An open orbit ring with a satellite riding in its gap. Use on surface, surface-raised or deep.
- `orbitra-mark-ink.svg`: one-colour ink version for print, stamps and places where orange is not available.
- `orbitra-app-icon.svg`: the app and developer-profile icon, solar mark on a deep rounded square (512 × 512, ready for the Play Console developer icon).
- `orbitra-lockup.svg`: mark plus the lowercase "orbitra" wordmark in Space Grotesk 600. The wordmark is live text: convert it to outlines in a design tool before sending it to print or third parties.

Clear space around the mark: at least the diameter of the satellite dot on every side. Minimum size: 16px for the mark, 96px wide for the lockup. Never rotate the ring, close the gap, change the dot's position, or add gradients and shadows.
